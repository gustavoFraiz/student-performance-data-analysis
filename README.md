# Student Performance Data Analysis

Exploratory data analysis and machine-learning study of the UCI **Student
Performance** dataset.

The notebook investigates demographic, social and academic variables and their
relationship with student outcomes. It includes descriptive statistics,
univariate and multivariate analysis, visualization and predictive modeling.

Examples of questions explored in the recovered notebook include:

- relationship between alcohol consumption and absences;
- rural vs. urban residence and final grades;
- family structure and absences;
- study time and grades;
- absences and final academic performance.
